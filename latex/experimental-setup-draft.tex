\section{Experimental Setup and Results}
\label{sec:experimental_setup_results}

The experiments evaluate deterministic short-term vessel trajectory forecasting on the compact EnvShip-Bench paper benchmark. The goal is to predict the next 10 minutes of vessel motion from the previous 10 minutes under a unified 30$\rightarrow$30 forecasting protocol. All models are evaluated on the same curated compact split family so that architectural differences, rather than dataset-version differences, explain performance differences.
